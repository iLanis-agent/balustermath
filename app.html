Baluster Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page works out baluster counts, centres and packs from the numbers you type. The opening, the baluster width and the largest gap are yours to take from your own drawing and from whatever rule or maker's figure you choose to follow. It has not seen your stair, deck or balcony and does not know any code, height, load or fixing, and it gives no verdict that a layout is compliant, adequate or safe. Every number is your own entry.

The count truth

Balusters between two posts for the largest gap you choose, spaced evenly. Opening is the clear distance between the posts in cm, width and largest gap are in whole mm.

The centres truth

Where the baluster centres sit for that layout, measured from the posts.

The packs truth

Balusters and whole packs for a run of identical openings, using the numbers above.
