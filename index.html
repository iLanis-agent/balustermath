Boundary: arithmetic only Baluster Math works out baluster counts, centres and packs from the numbers you type. The opening, the baluster width and the largest gap are yours to take from your own drawing and from whatever rule or maker's figure you choose to follow. It has not seen your stair, deck or balcony and does not know any code, height, load or fixing, and it gives no verdict that a layout is compliant, adequate or safe. Every number is your own entry.

Starting examples: a 180 cm opening, where the centres sit, and the packs for four such openings.
